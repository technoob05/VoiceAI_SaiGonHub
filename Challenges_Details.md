Designing a Future Where Language Is No Longer a Barrier
The problem
Technology exists, but communication still breaks down
In today’s global economy, language is no longer just culture; it determines access to opportunity.

Across factories, construction sites, logistics hubs, hospitals, and service environments, workers from different countries must collaborate every day. Productivity, skills transfer, workplace safety, and career mobility increasingly depend on the ability to communicate clearly and instantly across languages.

Yet communication inequality remains a hidden barrier. The reality today:

EdgeVoice Challenge
📊
Only 5% of Vietnamese workers are proficient in English, lower than many non-English speaking ASEAN countries

ManpowerGroup, 2022

💼
87% FDI companies require English or foreign language proficiency from employees

Phan & Dang (2024), IJMCER

⏰
Years to learn: Language fluency requires long-term training that many workers cannot afford

🤝
Gestures or mixed-language communication are often relied on, increasing misunderstanding and risk

📡
Connectivity barriers: Most translation tools rely on stable internet or cloud processing, which conditions are not always available in real-world environments

The challenge
Designing communication without interruption
OneVoice AI Challenge calls on innovators to rethink how humans communicate across languages.

Teams are invited to design standalone AI translation devices powered entirely on-device using Edge AI.

The goal: Enable natural bilingual conversations with minimal latency, even in noisy, hands-busy, or low-connectivity environments.

Teams will design and prototype devices that combine:

Edge AI models

On-device ML combining speech recognition, translation, and synthesis - no cloud dependency, optimized for speed and accuracy.

User-centered design

Intuitive device interaction designed for workers in factories, construction sites, and logistics centers, with minimal training required.

Note: Contestants are encouraged to use models from the Qualcomm AI Hub, which contains hundreds of models (like ControlNet, Stable Diffusion, and Llama) that have already been "pre-optimized" (quantized and compiled) to run perfectly on Snapdragon platforms.

Technical Requirements
Solutions may be deployed on any portable device format, without restriction to predefined device categories.

Core specifications: Solutions will be evaluated against the following technical benchmarks:
Innovation & Novelty (25%): Evaluates the problem-solving approach and the effectiveness of supplementary features.

Technical Performance (50%): Assesses core on-device system metrics, including accuracy, duration, speed, and latency.

Business Viability (25%): Evaluates the potential for productization and commercialization.

Technical specifications

Specific benchmark values will be published prior to the prototype submission phase. The final round will feature live demonstrations and winner announcement, where shortlisted teams present their working prototypes to a panel of judges.

Judging criteria
Solutions will be evaluated based on 3 core dimensions:

🚀 Innovation & Novelty - 25%
We celebrate ideas that challenge conventions and introduce new ways to bridge language barriers. Judges will look at how creatively teams approach the problem, the originality of the solution, and meaningful features that enhance real-world usability.

⚙️ Technical Excellence - 50%
At the heart of the challenge lies reliable Edge AI performance. Teams will be assessed on the effectiveness of their on-device translation system, including: translation accuracy, processing speed and responsiveness, low latency communication, and stability and operational performance.

🌍 Business Impact & Real-World Potential - 25%
Judges will evaluate how feasible each solution is for real deployment, including its practicality, scalability, and potential to evolve into a commercially viable product that benefits workers and organizations at scale.

Additional eligibility conditions
The submitted solution must be an original development created specifically for this competition.

Teams must not have an already commercialized product that is substantially similar to the competition submission.

Products that are currently on the market, revenue-generating, or in mass production are not eligible.

Early-stage prototypes or pre-commercial concepts are allowed.
